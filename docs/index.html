PaperDuet

A tool for researchers to manage, study and understand papers!

PaperDuet helps you manage, study and understand every paper you read.
Six open-source AI translation models run on your computer, or connect an online API. Free to use, no sign-up needed!

Open it — in your language

PaperDuet is a tool for managing, studying and understanding papers: the original and its translation sit side by side as mirror images, with formulas, figures and tables lined up page by page, and the papers you have read are kept on a shelf and in a Knowledge Space where AI finds them again. PaperDuet is free to use: with a local model everything runs on your computer, and you can connect an online API when you want to.

Knowledge Space

Your library is your night sky

Introducing Space: your papers now spread out before you like a night sky, so you can build a visual knowledge base of your own and see it at a glance.

Shelf and reading time

Every bit of progress, recorded

The shelf on the home page keeps count of how many papers you have read, for how long in all, and how many days in a row. Each square is a week, darker the longer you read; click a field and the papers and the timeline below follow it. A right-click puts your own labels on a paper.

AI Search

Can’t find a paper? Ask the AI!

No need to remember the title: say in a sentence what you are looking for, and AI Search finds it in titles, originals, translations and summaries, and shows the passage that matched. Ask in your own language or in English.

Mirror layout

Every page, just as it was

The original on the left, the translation on the right; both scroll and zoom together. Columns, headings, captions, footnotes and references are recognised by themselves, and formulas, figures and tables stay where they were; point at a paragraph and its counterpart on the other side lights up. Switch to another translation language and the layout stays the same.

Notes on the translation

Taking notes? Sorted!

Highlight, underline and write notes right on the translation; the matching sentence of the original is marked by itself. The original exported with your notes carries standard PDF annotations, so Preview, Zotero and other apps show them as they are.

Save and summarize

Finished? Take it with you.

When the whole paper is translated, the ring in the toolbar turns into a green check: one click saves the translated PDF, the Markdown and the annotated original together into the paper's own folder. The light bulb beside it is the AI summary, written by the model once it has read the whole paper: the question, the method and the results at a glance.

And there's more

  • Ten languages to translate into.

    Switch between 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Polski and Русский in one click from the toolbar.

  • Double-click to jump the queue.

    Want a later paragraph first? Double-click it in the original and it is translated next, without waiting for the progress to get there.

  • Quality checks built in.

    Formulas, numbers and sentence order are checked in every reply, with automatic retries, and a glossary for each paper keeps terms consistent.

  • You decide what gets translated.

    Right-click any block to translate it or keep the original.

  • Read your way.

    Search the original and the translation at once, browse a bilingual outline, point at a paragraph to find its twin, and show the original, the translation or both. PaperDuet remembers where you left off.

  • Your own terms.

    Tell PaperDuet how you want a term translated, and the whole paper follows it.

  • Scans can be read too.

    PDFs whose text is drawn as shapes rather than stored as text are read with the system's own text recognition on a Mac and on Windows, then translated.

  • Folders that keep up to date.

    Import a folder once, and papers put into it later show up in your library by themselves.

  • One click to update, one to clean up.

    A new version installs with one click; the cache is cleared with one click in Settings.

  • Made to look good.

    A clean, beautiful glass design, with light, dark and night reading modes.

Six models, all on your computer

Pick a model the first time you open PaperDuet, and switch anytime in Settings › Model › Change Model. Every one of them is built for translation and runs fully offline once it is downloaded.

  • Index-Translate-2Brecommended
    bilibili Index team · 2.1 GB

    The best all-rounder, tuned for PaperDuet's checks and glossary

  • Hy-MT2-1.8B
    Tencent Hunyuan · 1.9 GB

    Fast and light

  • LMT-60-4B
    NiuTrans · 4.7 GB

    Translations that stay close to the source

  • LMT-60-0.6B
    NiuTrans · 0.8 GB

    Modest computers: the smallest and fastest

  • Hy-MT2-7B
    Tencent Hunyuan · 8.0 GB

    The most fluent Chinese, on computers with 16 GB of memory or more

  • TranslateGemma-4Bexperimental
    Google · 4.1 GB

    Trying Google's translation model

With the recommended model, a 15-page paper takes about 2 minutes on an M5 Pro, or about 1 minute with "Paragraphs at Once" set to 2 or 3 in Settings › Model. Already have one of these models from LM Studio, llama.cpp or Hugging Face? PaperDuet finds it and uses it where it is, with no second download.

Computer too slow for a local model? Pick Use an API in the model choice and enter your own API key to use OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Doubao or any OpenAI-compatible service. The local models fold away, and translation, summaries, AI Organize and AI Search all go through that service. Your key stays on your computer.

Private by design

  • Your papers, translations and notes stay on your computer. With an API, the text to translate is sent to the service you chose.
  • Only if you agree, PaperDuet sends anonymous usage and diagnostic data (version, system, interface language, model and kinds of errors), never paper content, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings.
  • When something goes wrong, PaperDuet sends an error report to the developer: what went wrong, the version and the system, without file paths or paper content. You can turn this off in Settings › Data.
  • When you write feedback in the app and click Send, what you wrote (and your email, if you give one) goes to the developer with the version and the system.
  • With a local model, PaperDuet goes online only to download a model, to check for updates, to send the data and reports above and to send your feedback; you can turn off all but the model download.

Get started

A Mac needs Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13.3 or later; a PC needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11; Linux needs 64-bit Ubuntu 22.04 or later (or a similarly recent distribution).

Mac

  1. 1

    Download the latest .dmg from Releases.

  2. 2

    Drag PaperDuet into Applications and open it from there.

  3. 3

    First launch only: PaperDuet isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. (Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PaperDuet.app in Terminal.)

  4. 4

    Pick a model. PaperDuet downloads it, or finds the copy you already have, and starts translating right away.

Windows

  1. 1

    Download the latest Windows installer (_x64-setup.exe) from Releases.

  2. 2

    Run it. First time only: if Windows says “Windows protected your PC,” click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Pick a model. PaperDuet downloads it, or finds the copy you already have, and starts translating right away.

Linux

  1. 1

    Download the latest .deb (Ubuntu, Debian) or .AppImage (other distributions, or without administrator rights) from Releases.

  2. 2

    .deb: run sudo apt install ./PaperDuet_*_amd64.deb in a terminal, then open PaperDuet from the applications menu. .AppImage: nothing to install; run chmod +x PaperDuet_*_amd64.AppImage, then open it.

  3. 3

    Pick a model. PaperDuet downloads it, or finds the copy you already have, and starts translating right away.

That's all. The translation engine is built into the app, and updates take one click.

FAQ

Which model should I choose?

Start with Index-Translate-2B. For more speed, pick Hy-MT2-1.8B. For the most fluent Chinese on a computer with 16 GB of memory or more, pick Hy-MT2-7B. If your computer struggles, pick Use an API.

Is it free?

Yes, for personal study and research, and for universities, schools, research institutes and other non-profits. Using PaperDuet at or for a company needs a commercial license: write to support@paperduet.com.
