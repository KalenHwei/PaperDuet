Translating Attention Is All You Need (15 pages) into Simplified Chinese, from scratch.

PaperDuet is a lightweight AI mirror translator for papers. It lays out the original and the translation as mirror images, with formulas, figures and tables corrected and aligned page by page. No sign-up, no API key, no fees, and it runs entirely on your computer!

Mirror layout

Every page, made easy

The original paper is on the left and its translation on the right, and both sides scroll and zoom together. Columns, headings, captions, footnotes and references are recognised automatically, and paragraphs that run across columns or pages are joined up. The two sides line up exactly, so comparing them is easy.

Save and summarize

Saving translation? AI summary? No problem!

The toolbar shows the translation progress. When the whole paper is done, the ring turns into a green check and a bubble pops up: one click saves the translation as a PDF next to the original. The light bulb next to it is the AI summary: open it to read what the paper asks, how it works and what it found, written by the model after reading the whole paper. You can also choose from five models to suit your computer and your needs!

Organize and search

Think it. Find it.

Every paper you've read lives in your library. AI Organize tags each one by field and topic, and the papers you pick rise to the top. AI Search finds a paper by its title, its text, its translation or its summary, in English or Chinese.

Jump the queue

Don't get it? Go click it.

Headings are translated first, so you can see how the paper is structured; then the title, abstract and introduction. Want a later passage now? Double-click it and it jumps the queue, no waiting for the progress to get there.

Notes on the translation

Taking notes? You got it!

Highlight, underline and write notes right on the translation; the matching sentences in the original are marked too. Export the original PDF with your notes as standard PDF annotations, ready for Preview, Zotero and other apps.

And there's more

  • Eight languages to translate into.

    Switch between 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español and Русский in one click from the toolbar.

  • A translated PDF in one click.

    Once a paper is fully translated, one click saves the translation next to it in the original layout.

  • Quality checks built in.

    Formulas, numbers and sentence order are checked in every reply, with automatic retries, and a glossary for each paper keeps terms consistent.

  • You decide what gets translated.

    Right-click any block to translate it or keep the original.

  • Read your way.

    Search the original and the translation at once, browse a bilingual outline, point at a paragraph to find its twin, and show the original, the translation or both. PaperDuet remembers where you left off.

  • Your library, with AI search.

    Every paper you read or import is in the library. AI Search finds one again by its title, text, translation or summary, in English or Chinese, and AI Organize sorts papers by field and topic.

  • A summary of every paper.

    Once a paper is translated, click the light bulb: the translation model writes a short summary in your interface language and keeps it with the paper.

  • Export anywhere.

    The original PDF with your marks, the translated PDF and your notes as Markdown, all readable in other apps too.

  • One-click updates.

    Updating is easy: one click installs the new version.

  • Made to look good.

    A clean, beautiful glass design, with light, dark and night reading modes.

Five models, all on your computer

Pick a model the first time you open PaperDuet, and switch anytime in Settings › Model › Change Model. Every one of them is built for translation and runs fully offline once it is downloaded.

  • Index-Translate-2Brecommended
    bilibili Index team · 2.1 GB

    The best all-rounder, tuned for PaperDuet's checks and glossary

  • Hy-MT2-1.8B
    Tencent Hunyuan · 1.9 GB

    Speed: the fastest and lightest

  • LMT-60-4B
    NiuTrans · 4.7 GB

    Translations that stay close to the source

  • Hy-MT2-7B
    Tencent Hunyuan · 8.0 GB

    The most fluent Chinese, on computers with 16 GB of memory or more

  • TranslateGemma-4Bexperimental
    Google · 4.1 GB

    Trying Google's translation model

With the recommended model, a 15-page paper takes about 2 minutes on an M5 Pro, or about 1 minute with "Paragraphs at Once" set to 2 or 3 in Settings › Model. Already have one of these models from LM Studio, llama.cpp or Hugging Face? PaperDuet finds it and uses it where it is, with no second download.

Private by design

  • Your papers, translations and notes stay on your computer.
  • No account needed. Only if you agree, PaperDuet sends anonymous usage and diagnostic data (version, system, interface language, model and kinds of errors), never paper content, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings.
  • PaperDuet goes online only to download a model, to check for updates and to send that data; you can turn off the last two.

Get started

A Mac needs Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13.3 or later; a PC needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

Mac

  1. 1

    Download the latest .dmg from Releases.

  2. 2

    Drag PaperDuet into Applications and open it from there.

  3. 3

    First launch only: PaperDuet isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. (Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PaperDuet.app in Terminal.)

  4. 4

    Pick a model. PaperDuet downloads it, or finds the copy you already have, and starts translating right away.

Windows

  1. 1

    Download the latest Windows installer (_x64-setup.exe) from Releases.

  2. 2

    Run it. First time only: if Windows says “Windows protected your PC,” click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Pick a model. PaperDuet downloads it, or finds the copy you already have, and starts translating right away.

That's all. The translation engine is built into the app, and updates take one click.

FAQ

Which model should I choose?

Start with Index-Translate-2B. For more speed, pick Hy-MT2-1.8B. For the most fluent Chinese on a computer with 16 GB of memory or more, pick Hy-MT2-7B.

Is it free?

Yes, for personal study and research, and for universities, schools, research institutes and other non-profits. Using PaperDuet at or for a company needs a commercial license: write to junystugh@gmail.com.
